Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. [OPERATOR NAME] operates Sentinel.

What we collect

Your email address, optional name, a bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), the files you upload, and an activity log of actions on your account (sign-ins, uploads, downloads, shares) including IP addresses. We use one session cookie to keep you signed in; no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To run the Service, keep it secure, prevent abuse, and show you your own activity. We do not sell your data or use your files for advertising.

How your files are protected

Files are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, each with its own key. The server holds the master key needed to decrypt files when you or a link recipient downloads them, so this is not end-to-end encryption, and the operator could technically access file contents. We do not do so except where required to investigate abuse or comply with the law.

Sharing

Files are shared only through links you create. Anyone with a link (and its password, if set) can download until it expires or you revoke it. We use a hosting provider [HOSTING PROVIDER] to run the Service.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account in Account settings erases your files, links and activity records. Backups made before deletion may persist for up to 14 days.

Your rights and contact

You can view, correct and delete your data in the app. For anything else, or to report a problem, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].
