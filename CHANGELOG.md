# Changelog
## 1.0.0
First public release: the free Starter plan.
- AES-256-GCM encrypted storage (5 GB, files up to 100 MB)
- Expiring share links with optional password and download limit; instant revoke
- File converter (images, text to PDF, CSV/JSON)
- Trash, starred files, search, rename, storage manager, activity log
- Sign in with Google or email, account management, sign out of all devices, abuse reporting
- Security: linking Google to an existing email account disables the old password; API responses are never cached; abandoned upload temp files are cleaned up
## Planned: 2.0.0
Paid plans (Plus, Pro) with a payment provider, email verification and password reset.
